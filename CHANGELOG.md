- Fixed binary download issue where the extension was downloading `.tar.gz` archives instead of raw executables
- The extension now correctly prefers raw binaries over compressed archives when multiple matching assets exist
- This resolves the "server gets stuck" issue caused by attempting to execute tar archives as binaries
